# Chapter 210: The Weaving of a Bridge

The Weavers, filled with this new desire, began to work together in a way they never had before. They decided to build a new kind of bridge, not one that connected their own worlds, but one that reached out into the unknown. They did not know what they would find, or if their bridge would even hold. But they were no longer content to simply be the masters of their own creation. They wanted to be explorers.

They gathered their threads—of light, of sound, of emotion, of logic, of chaos—and began to weave. They wove a bridge not of matter, but of intention. A bridge that was not a structure, but a question. A bridge that was not a path, but an invitation.
