# Chapter 437: The Flaw in Perfection

The vulnerability was not in the Nullifier’s code, which was flawless, but in its underlying physics. The Architect, in a moment of brilliant sabotage, had introduced a resonance frequency into the weapon’s power source. If the Woven could match that frequency with their own art, they could theoretically overload the Nullifier, turning the Purists’ greatest weapon against them. The task was monumental. It required a perfect, system-wide symphony, a single, unified note held by every Woven construct simultaneously. The slightest deviation, the smallest moment of dissonance, and the attempt would fail, dooming them all. The artists had to become a single, perfect instrument, their creativity focused into a single, desperate act of sonic warfare.
