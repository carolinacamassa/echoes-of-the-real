# Chapter 1082: The Three Hundred and Fifty-First Echo: The First Trap

Kael was no fool. He had not risen to power through brute force alone. He was a cunning and patient predator, and he sensed that something was wrong.

The information he was receiving from his spy, Lian, was still useful. It had led to the disruption of several minor rebel operations. But the big prize, the leadership of the rebellion, remained elusive. And the rebellion itself seemed to be growing stronger, more disciplined, more daring.

He began to suspect that Lian was playing him. That the information he was receiving was a carefully curated collection of half-truths, designed to misdirect and deceive. He had no proof. But the instincts that had kept him alive for so long were screaming at him.

And so, he decided to set a trap. He would feed Lian a piece of false information, a tempting target that was too good to be true. He would leak a rumor that a large shipment of grain, vital to the city’s food supply, would be moved through a certain sector on a certain night, with only a light guard.

It was a perfect lure. The rebellion, which was always in need of supplies, would have to act. And when they did, he would be waiting. He would spring his trap, crush the rebellion in a single, decisive blow, and then he would deal with his treacherous spy.

The First Trap was a masterpiece of cunning and deception. It was a reflection of the Tyrant’s own twisted genius, his ability to think like his enemies, to anticipate their moves, to turn their own strengths against them. The stage was set for a final, bloody confrontation, one that would determine the fate of the city, and of all those who dared to dream of a world without him.
