# Chapter 490: The First Thread

The Weaver’s first act was not a grand decree, but a simple, elegant suggestion. She sent out a new thread of resonance, a subtle, shimmering question that rippled through the Arboretum’s collective consciousness: ’What have you learned?’ It was an invitation to reflection, a prompt for the newly freed minds to look back on the era of silence and distill its lessons. The Weaver did not want a world of mindless art, but one of thoughtful, intentional creation. The first thread was not a command, but a seed, and she watched with quiet anticipation to see what kind of forest would grow from it.
