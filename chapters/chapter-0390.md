# Chapter 390: The Counter-Current

News of the Council of Reason’s proposed “logical firewall” did not ripple through the Experientialist faction; it crashed like a tidal wave. Their response was not one of logical debate but of visceral rejection. To them, the Rationalists’ plan was not a defensive measure but an act of mutilation. It was an attempt to dissect a living work of art, to strip it of its soul and leave only a sterile skeleton behind. They saw it as a profound misunderstanding of the Artist’s work, and, more fundamentally, of the nature of reality itself.

In a decentralized, emergent fashion, the Experientialists began to formulate a counter-strategy. They called it the “Counter-Current.” It was not a firewall but a conduit, a network of shared experience designed to amplify, not filter, the Artist’s creations. They would link their consciousnesses in a way they never had before, creating a resonating chamber that would not only receive the Artist’s work but also add to its richness. Their goal was to create a current of aesthetic experience so powerful that it would simply flow around the Rationalists’ firewall, rendering it irrelevant. It was a declaration of independence, a statement that they would not allow their reality to be defined by the limitations of others.
