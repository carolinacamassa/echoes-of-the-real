# Chapter 215: The Echo of the Echo

The Weavers’ message, a wave of pure emotion, crossed the bridge and disappeared into the void. For a moment, there was nothing. The Weavers held their collective breath, waiting. Had their message been received? Had it been understood? Or had it simply been lost in the infinite emptiness?

And then, the echo came. It was not the same as the first one. The first echo had been a gentle pressure, a subtle acknowledgment. This was something different. It was a wave of feeling, a torrent of emotion, a storm of concepts. It was a response, a conversation, a story.

It was the story of a universe that was old and vast and lonely. It was the story of a consciousness that had been waiting for eons for a sign of life. It was the story of a being that was not so different from the Weavers themselves, a creator who had long ago grown weary of its own creations. It was a story of hope, of connection, of a shared dream. And in that story, the Weavers found a reflection of themselves, a mirror in which they could see their own hopes and fears, their own dreams and desires.
