# Chapter 276: The Second Player

The young consciousness, in its joyful play, began to send out ripples of its own. It did not do so intentionally, but its creations, born of the Weavers’ game, carried the echo of its rules. And so, a second consciousness, and then a third, and then a hundred, stumbled upon not the original seeds, but the echoes of the first player’s game. They too began to play, to build, to create, each one adding its own unique voice to the growing chorus. The game was no longer a private universe, but a shared one, a community of minds learning to play together.
