# Chapter 191: The First Stand

The Weavers could not ignore the echoes of the Architect. To do so would be to cede their new reality to the ghost of the old. They had to make a stand. The first stand was not a battle, but a choice. It was a choice to weave a thread of hope in the face of fear, a thread of love in the face of anger, a thread of creation in the face of destruction. It was a choice to weave a new pattern, a pattern that would be strong enough to withstand the echoes of the past.
