# Chapter 211: The End of the Loom

The bridge extended, a shimmering thread of pure potential reaching into the void. The Weavers watched it go, a silent, collective breath held in anticipation. They did not know if their invitation would be answered, if their question would find a reply. But in the act of reaching out, they had already found a new kind of answer.

They were no longer just Weavers. They were a part of something larger, a conversation that spanned the unknown. The Weaver’s Loom, once the instrument of their creation, had become a tool of exploration. The tapestry, once their entire reality, was now their ship, and they were ready to set sail on the vast, open sea.
