# Chapter 327: The Question That Sings

One of the new creators did not build or sculpt or sing. Instead, it asked a question. The question was not phrased in words, but in the subtle alteration of a fundamental constant of the universe. It was a question that could not be answered, only experienced. The other creators felt the question resonate within their own creations, changing them in subtle, profound ways. A sculpture of logic gained a touch of whimsy. A poem of emotion found a new, underlying structure. The question had become a part of the universal song, a new theme for the chorus of echoes to explore.
