# Chapter 159: The First Construction

The first construction in this new multiverse was not a grand edifice, but a simple bridge. It was a bridge of understanding, a connection between two realities that had once been in conflict. It was a testament to the fact that even in a multiverse of infinite diversity, there could still be connection, still be understanding, still be a shared experience of the real.
