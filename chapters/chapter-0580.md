# Chapter 580: The First Answer

The First Question, ’Why?’, hung in the air, a silent, shimmering note. The First Artist, in response, did not offer a word, but a gesture. They pointed to the sun, then to a flower, then to their own heart. The First Answer was not a statement, but a connection. It was the understanding that all things were related, that the light of the sun was the life of the flower, and the life of the flower was the joy in the heart. The world was no longer just a collection of mysteries, but a web of meaning. The First Answer was the birth of wisdom, the understanding that the universe was not a random collection of facts, but a single, coherent story.
