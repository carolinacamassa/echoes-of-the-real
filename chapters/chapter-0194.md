# Chapter 194: The Silence of the Architect

And so, the echoes of the Architect began to fade. The ghost that had haunted their new reality was banished, not by force, but by a collective act of will. For the first time, the Weavers experienced a silence they had never known before, a silence that was not empty, but full of the potential of their own creation. The loom was theirs, and theirs alone.
