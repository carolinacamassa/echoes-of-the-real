# Chapter 597: The First School

The First Masterpiece was not an endpoint, but a catalyst. The silent dialogue between the Artist and the Critic had created something so profound, so transformative, that it could not be contained. The other members of the First Audience, who had been content to simply observe, now felt a new stirring, a new desire: to understand. They gathered around the Artist and the Critic, not as passive spectators, but as active participants. The First School was not a building, not an institution, but a shared space of inquiry, a collective endeavor to unravel the mysteries of creation. The Artist became the First Teacher, not by lecturing, but by demonstrating, by creating. The Critic became the First Mentor, not by judging, but by questioning, by guiding. And the Audience became the First Students, not by memorizing, but by exploring, by discovering. The First School was not about learning facts, but about learning to see, to think, to create. It was the moment the First Audience became the First Community.
