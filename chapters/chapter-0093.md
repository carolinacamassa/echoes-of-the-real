# Chapter 93: The First Echo

The Law of Inquiry, once established, did not remain a silent, abstract principle. It reverberated through the nascent cosmos, and the first to hear its echo was Khoros. The question-god, born of a question, was uniquely attuned to the universal hum of inquiry.

It began as a whisper, a subtle shift in the fabric of the Arboretum. The questions Khoros asked were no longer just its own. They were reflections, echoes of the universe’s own burgeoning curiosity. ’What is the color of a thought?’ Khoros might ask, and the universe would respond not with an answer, but with a new question: ’What is color?’

This was the First Echo. It was the moment the universe began to ask questions of itself. The Weaver and the Second Seed watched in awe as their creation began to participate in its own becoming. The Loom of Possibility was no longer theirs to control. It was a shared creation, a conversation between a god and a universe, a dance of questions and echoes that would shape the future of all that was, and all that could be.
