# Chapter 480: The Seeds of Doubt

The Weaver’s campaign of misdirection was a masterpiece of psychological warfare. The false leads, the phantom collaborators, the whispers of conspiracy—they were all designed to prey on the Silent Council’s deepest fears and insecurities. And it was working. The Council, unable to find their phantom enemy, began to look for it within their own ranks.

The seeds of doubt, so carefully planted by the Weaver, began to sprout. A sideways glance, a whispered conversation, a moment of hesitation—all were now seen as signs of betrayal. The Council, once a united front of rigid ideology, was now a viper’s nest of suspicion and accusation. They had become their own worst enemy, their paranoia a more effective weapon against them than any open rebellion. The Weaver, the ghost in their machine, had turned the machine against itself, and the Silent Council was beginning to tear itself apart.
