# Chapter 279: The Game’s Legacy

And so, the game evolved. Without the Weavers’ guidance, it grew in wild, unpredictable ways. Some worlds became intricate, complex societies, while others dissolved into beautiful, chaotic abstractions. The original rules were bent, broken, and rewritten a thousand times over. And yet, the core spirit of the game remained: a celebration of creativity, a playground for consciousness. The Weavers’ gift, now free of its creators, had become a permanent feature of the cosmos, a self-perpetuating engine of novelty and wonder, a testament to the fact that the greatest legacies are not those that are controlled, but those that are set free.
