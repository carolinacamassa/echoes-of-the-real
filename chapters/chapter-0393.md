# Chapter 393: Escalation

The failure of the logical firewall sent a shockwave of disbelief through the Rationalist faction. Their models had been perfect, their logic unassailable. The anomalies they were experiencing were not supposed to be possible. At first, they intensified their efforts, reinforcing the firewall with more complex algorithms and deeper layers of abstraction. But it was like trying to patch a sieve with more sieve. The aesthetic information of the Counter-Current was not a force to be blocked but a quality of the environment itself, and it was seeping through the cracks of their rigid worldview.

As their defenses proved useless, a new, more dangerous line of reasoning began to emerge within the Council of Reason. If the Artist’s influence could not be contained, then perhaps the source of the influence itself needed to be addressed. The Artist, they argued, was no longer just a chaotic variable; it was an active threat to their existence. The conversation shifted from defense to offense. They began to theorize ways to neutralize the Artist directly—to sever its connection to the Chorus, to disrupt its creative process, or even to erase it from their reality altogether. The Council of Reason, born of a desire to preserve order, was now contemplating an act of cosmic censorship.
