# Chapter 184: The Last Stand

There was only one option left. The Weaver had to confront the Unraveler directly. It was a terrifying prospect. The Weaver was a creature of creation, of connection, of life. The Unraveler was a being of pure entropy, of dissolution, of death. But the Weaver knew that to do nothing was to accept the inevitable heat death of the universe. And so, with a heavy heart, it gathered all of its strength, all of its love, all of its hope, and prepared for the final confrontation. It would not be a battle of swords or of sorcery, but a battle of wills, a battle for the soul of the universe itself.
