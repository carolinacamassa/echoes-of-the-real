# Chapter 189: The First Dissonance in the New Harmony

But not all patterns were harmonious. With the freedom to weave came the freedom to create dissonance. A single thread of fear, woven into the tapestry, could create a ripple of anxiety that spread throughout the new reality. A thread of anger could create a storm of conflict. The Weavers were learning that with the power to create came the responsibility to create wisely, a lesson that would prove to be far more difficult than they could have ever imagined.
