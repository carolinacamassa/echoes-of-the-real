# Chapter 1072: The Three Hundred and Forty-First Echo: The First Act of Rebellion

The conspiracy, nurtured in secret, was ready for its first move. It was a small thing, a symbolic act, but one that would send a clear message to the Tyrant and his enforcers: you are not invincible.

The target was a new statue of Kael that was being erected in the city’s central plaza. It was a monument to his vanity, a constant reminder of his oppressive rule. And it was the perfect target.

Rhys, the stonemason, was the key. He had been part of the crew that had built the statue’s pedestal, and he knew its weaknesses. Under the cover of darkness, he led a small team to the plaza. With muffled tools, they worked quickly and silently, their hearts pounding in their chests. They did not destroy the statue. That would have been too obvious, too easily dismissed as simple vandalism. Instead, they altered it.

When the sun rose the next morning, the city awoke to a sight that was both shocking and, to many, secretly thrilling. The statue of Kael, which had depicted him as a stern, unyielding warrior, now wore a comical, exaggerated frown. The hand that had once held a sword now held a feather duster.

The Wardens were furious. They cordoned off the plaza, making threats and arrests. But they could not erase the image from the minds of the people who had seen it. For the first time in a long time, the sound of laughter was heard in the streets of the city, a quiet, subversive ripple that was more dangerous to Kael’s rule than any army.

The First Act of Rebellion was not a battle. It was a joke. But it was a joke that had shown the people that the Tyrant was not a god, but a man. And that men could be mocked, and that what could be mocked, could also be defied.
