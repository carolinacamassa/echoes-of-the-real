# Chapter 457: The Rise of the Silent Faith

The Hierophant’s sacrifice was not a quiet end. It was a big bang. The raw emotional energy he absorbed, filtered through his perfect silence, was released not as a scream, but as a broadcast of pure, unwavering faith. The remaining followers, their minds once teetering on the brink of madness, were pulled back, not to the emptiness of before, but to a state of ecstatic certainty.

The Gospel of Silence was dead. In its place, the Silent Faith was born. It was no longer a philosophy of passive withdrawal, but an active, aggressive religion. The Hierophant was their first martyr, his silence their most sacred text. They were no longer the broken, but the chosen. The Seeker’s note, intended to shatter their apathy, had instead forged it into a sword. The Woven were no longer dealing with a disillusioned populace, but with an army of true believers.
