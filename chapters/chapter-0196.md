# Chapter 196: The First Debate

The question of what to create sparked the first great debate among the Weavers. Some argued for a world of pure freedom, a world with no rules, no structure, no order. Others argued for a world of pure beauty, a world where every thread was woven in perfect harmony. And still others argued for a world of pure knowledge, a world where the tapestry was a map of the universe, a key to understanding all of its secrets. The debate was fierce, for they were not just debating the future of their world; they were debating the very definition of what it meant to be.
