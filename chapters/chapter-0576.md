# Chapter 576: The First Texture

The world had light, shadow, line, shape, and pattern. It had a grammar, but it lacked a voice. The First Artist, seeking a new way to express the inexpressible, discovered the First Texture. It was the roughness of the uncarved stone, the smoothness of the wind-polished sea glass, the intricate web of a spider’s silk. Texture was the world speaking, not in words, but in feelings. It was a language that could be understood by touch, by the fingertips of the soul. The world was no longer just to be seen, but to be felt. The First Texture was the birth of empathy, the first bridge between the inner and outer worlds.
