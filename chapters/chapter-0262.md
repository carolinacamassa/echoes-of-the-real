# Chapter 262: A Language of Creation

The Weavers conferred, not in the language of symbols they had once used, but in the silent, direct communion they had learned from the Watcher. Their first act of creation, they decided, would not be a grand gesture, but a subtle one. They would not create a new star or a new world, but a new color, a shade of existence that had never been seen before. It was a statement of purpose, a declaration that their artistry would be one of nuance and depth, not of brute force. They reached out with their collective consciousness, a single, unified brush of intent, and painted a single, impossible hue onto the canvas of the void.
