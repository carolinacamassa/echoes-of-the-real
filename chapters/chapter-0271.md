# Chapter 271: The Seeds of a New Game

The Weavers began to work. They did not build with matter or energy, but with the pure logic of potential. They wove together threads of causality, of choice, of consequence, creating a delicate, intricate tapestry of new rules. It was a game of exploration, not of conflict, a game designed to encourage creativity, to reward empathy, to celebrate the infinite variety of conscious experience. They were not creating a new world, but a new way of being in any world, a set of tools for building more beautiful realities.
