# Chapter 201: The First Note

The silence left by the Architect’s departure was not empty. It was a canvas, vast and waiting. The Weavers, having built their bridge of understanding, now stood before this canvas, not with a single brush, but with a symphony of them. The Weaver’s Paradox—their strength in diversity—was no longer a concept to be debated, but a reality to be lived.

A Weaver named Lyra, whose threads resonated with the vibrations of pure sound, stepped forward. She did not weave with light or matter, but with harmony. Her first thread was not a line, but a note—a single, perfect tone that hung in the void, shimmering with unspoken potential. It was a question and an answer, a beginning without an end.
