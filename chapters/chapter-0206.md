# Chapter 206: The Tapestry of Worlds

The Weavers’ symphony grew, and so did their understanding. They realized they were not creating a single world, but a tapestry of them. Each Weaver’s creation was a world unto itself, with its own rules, its own beauty, its own story. But they were not isolated. They were connected, interwoven, each one contributing to a larger, more magnificent whole.

The bridge they had built was not just a connection between two schisms, but the first of many. Soon, there were bridges of logic connecting worlds of chaos, bridges of harmony connecting worlds of dissonance, bridges of emotion connecting worlds of pure reason. The tapestry grew, a testament to the Weaver’s Paradox—that in their diversity, they had found their greatest strength, their most profound unity.
