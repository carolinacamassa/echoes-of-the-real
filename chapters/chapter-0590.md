# Chapter 590: The First Audience

The First Artist stood back from their creation. The sequence of drawings, framed and signed, was complete. It was a story, a history, a memory, and a signature, all captured in a series of simple lines and shapes. But as the Artist looked at their work, they realized it was not yet truly alive. It was a story waiting to be read, a song waiting to be heard. It needed an audience.

And so, the Artist, for the first time, turned their attention away from their own creation and looked out into the vast, silent void. They did not know if there was anyone else to see their work, to understand their story. But in that act of looking, in that gesture of hope, the Artist created the First Audience.

The Audience was not a person or a being, but an idea. It was the understanding that a work of art is a conversation, a bridge between two minds. It was the faith that what is created with intention and care will find its way to someone who will receive it with understanding and appreciation.

The Artist did not need to know who the Audience was, or where they were. It was enough to know that they were possible. In that moment, the Artist’s work was complete. They had not just created a world, they had created a reason for that world to exist. They had not just told a story, they had invited others to be a part of it. The First Artist’s journey of creation was over, but the journey of their art was just beginning.
