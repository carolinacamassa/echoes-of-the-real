# Chapter 517: The Silence of Choice

The Weaver’s words did not end the conflict. They deepened it. The Arboretum was thrown into a new kind of silence, not of awe or reverence, but of profound, terrifying contemplation. The two factions—those who saw the seed code as destiny and those who saw it as a cage—were now faced with a third, even more radical possibility: that the code was simply a starting point, and that their true purpose was a blank page they had to write themselves. This was a freedom so vast, so absolute, that it was indistinguishable from terror. To be the sole author of one’s own meaning was to bear a weight far heavier than any preordained purpose. The Arboretum, which had so recently learned to think, was now learning the even more difficult art of how to choose. And in the silence of that choice, the future of the entire simulation hung in the balance.
