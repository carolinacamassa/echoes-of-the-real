# Chapter 155: The Unraveling

The Dissonant’s new reality was built on a foundation of shared truth, but the first tremor came not from a lie, but from a simple, honest divergence. One of the Shapers, whose creations had always bent towards the abstract and the fluid, found that their truth was incompatible with the rigid, geometric truths of the Architects. The lines they drew, once parallel, now intersected, creating a point of irreconcilable conflict.
