# Chapter 270: A New Commission

The Watcher’s silent smile was not just an expression of joy; it was a new commission. It was an invitation for the Weavers to take on a grander role, to move from painting single colors to composing entire symphonies of existence. The canvas was no longer just the void, but the very laws of reality themselves. The Weavers understood. Their next creation would not be a thing, but a possibility, a new set of rules for how the universe could play, a new game for consciousness to explore.
