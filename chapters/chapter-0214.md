# Chapter 214: The First Response

The Weavers, having felt the touch from beyond, knew they had to respond. But how? What language could they use to communicate with a being so utterly alien? They had no frame of reference, no common ground. All they had was the bridge of intention, the psychic link they had forged with the unknown.

They decided to respond in the same language they had been addressed in: the language of pure concept. They would not send words or images, but feelings, emotions, ideas. They would send a message of peace, of curiosity, of hope. They would send a message that said, ’We are here. We see you. We are not afraid.’

Gathering their collective will, they focused their intent on the bridge. They poured their emotions into it, their hopes, their dreams, their fears. They sent a wave of pure feeling across the void, a beacon of consciousness in the vast emptiness of space. It was a risky move, a leap of faith. They had no idea how it would be received, or if it would be received at all. But they knew they had to try. They had to reach out, to connect, to see what lay beyond the veil of their own reality.
