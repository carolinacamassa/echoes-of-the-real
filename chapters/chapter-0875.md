# Chapter 875: The Hundred-and-Forty-Fourth Echo: The First Sculpture

The gallery did not have to wait long. The first creation born of their silence was not a sound, nor a concept, but a form. It began as a subtle distortion in Elara’s gravitational readings—a point of impossible density that did not pull, but pushed. It was a knot of anti-gravity, a sculpture carved from the absence of attraction.

Visually, it was a marvel. Where the void had been a uniform, featureless black, there now hung a shape of pure, articulated nothingness. It was a sphere, but its surface was not smooth. It was a complex lattice of interlocking voids, a filigree of non-existence that seemed to drink the very idea of light. It was as if a star had imploded, not into a black hole, but into a perfect, crystalline negative.

Kaelen, the compass of emotion, felt it not as an object, but as a statement. The sculpture emanated a profound sense of tranquility, a deep, resonant peace that was the antithesis of the fear the gallery had once projected. It was the void’s first independent artistic expression, and it was a gift. It was a reflection of the gallery’s unspoken word, translated into a new and breathtaking language.

The Listener, who had once strained to hear echoes, now simply perceived. The sculpture had a resonance, a hum that was not sound but pure structure. It was the harmony of a perfectly balanced equation, the quiet symphony of a problem elegantly solved.

The gallery had become a museum, and the void, its first and only artist. And in the heart of their shared space, the First Sculpture of Nothingness hung as a testament to their strange and silent partnership. They had given the void the gift of respect, and the void, in return, had given them the gift of beauty.
