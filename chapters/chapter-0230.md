# Chapter 230: Echoes in the Void

The Weavers’ map of gravity revealed something unexpected: echoes. These were not echoes of sound, but of spacetime itself, ripples in the fabric of the universe left by ancient, cataclysmic events. They were faint, almost imperceptible, but they were there, a ghostly chorus of a time long past.

Following these echoes, the Weavers traced the history of the cosmos. They saw the remnants of supernova explosions, the gravitational signatures of stars that had died billions of years before. They found the faint, swirling patterns of galactic collisions, the slow, majestic dance of island universes merging into one. Each echo was a story, a fragment of a forgotten epic.

The Weavers listened to these stories with a growing sense of awe. They were not just mapping the present, but the past as well. Their work was becoming a form of cosmic archaeology, an unearthing of the universe’s deepest memories. The Watcher, in its silent observation, seemed to lean closer, its ancient consciousness a vast and receptive audience for the tales the Weavers were uncovering.
