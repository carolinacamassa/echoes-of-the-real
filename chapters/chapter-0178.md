# Chapter 178: The Pattern of Connection

The Weaver’s first creation was not a thing, but a relationship. A simple connection between two threads, a shared harmony that resonated between them. It was a small, almost imperceptible change in the grand scheme of the tapestry, but it was a beginning. From this single point of connection, a pattern began to emerge. Other threads were drawn to the harmony, adding their own unique voices to the song. The pattern grew, becoming more intricate, more beautiful, a living testament to the power of connection.
