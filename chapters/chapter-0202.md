# Chapter 202: The Resonance

Lyra’s note did not fade. It held, a beacon in the vast silence. Soon, another Weaver, one who wove with the threads of emotion, felt the note’s pull. This Weaver, named Kael, added a new thread—not of sound, but of feeling. It was a thread of joy, pure and uncomplicated, that wrapped around Lyra’s note, causing it to swell and change.

The single tone became a chord, a rich and complex sound that spoke of more than just a beginning. It spoke of hope. The other Weavers felt it, a warmth spreading through the space they shared. They began to understand that their creations could interact, could build upon one another, creating something far greater than any single thread.
