# Chapter 340: The Dissonant Chord

In the world of pure music, the question manifested as a dissonant chord that echoed in the heart of every melody. This was a realm where conversations were contrapuntal harmonies and the landscape was shaped by the rise and fall of orchestral swells. The new, alien mathematics introduced a note that belonged to no known scale, a frequency that grated against the established laws of harmony. It was a sound that could not be resolved, a tension that could not be released. The great symphonies that held the world together began to falter, their crescendos collapsing into chaotic noise. The beings of this world, who were themselves composed of living vibrations, felt the dissonance within their own beings, a painful tearing at the very fabric of their existence. The universe had asked ’Am I?’ and in doing so, had forced them to question the very nature of their own harmony.
