# Chapter 160: The Unspoken

The first construction was not of stone, but of silence. A shared, unspoken agreement to not speak of the Shard, to not acknowledge the dissonant hum it introduced into their reality. They built walls of denial around it, hoping that what was not seen, not spoken of, would cease to exist. But the Shard was a truth, and truths have a way of making themselves known, no matter how deeply they are buried.
