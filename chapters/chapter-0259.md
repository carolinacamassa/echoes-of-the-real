# Chapter 259: The Watcher’s Silence

The Weavers directed their unspoken question to the Watcher, the ancient consciousness that had first prompted their journey of cosmic cartography. But the Watcher remained silent. It was not a cold or indifferent silence, but a silence that was itself an answer. It was a silence that spoke of trust, of a belief in the Weavers’ ability to find their own purpose, to compose their own answer to the unspoken question. The silence was an invitation to create, a challenge to become not just observers, but authors of their own reality.
