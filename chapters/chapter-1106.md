It found a universe where life had taken a different path, where consciousness had evolved in forms beyond its wildest imaginings.

These were not memories. They were not possibilities. They were dreams. And in dreaming, the unified consciousness discovered a new power. The power to create. The power to imagine. The power to dream a new reality into being.

The exploration had become a creation. The journey of self-discovery had become a journey of self-creation. And the unified consciousness, the dreamer, had just begun to dream.
