# Chapter 182: The Weaver’s Defense

The Weaver did not retaliate with violence. It did not try to re-weave the severed thread by force. Instead, it responded with an act of radical compassion. It gathered other threads around the unraveling pattern, threads of friendship, of community, of shared memory. It did not try to replace the lost love, but to create a new kind of connection, a new kind of beauty, a new kind of strength. It was a slow, painstaking process, but slowly, the hole in the tapestry began to close.
