# Chapter 277: Emergent Worlds

With each new player, the game grew more complex. It was no longer a single melody, but a symphony, with harmonies and counter-melodies that the Weavers had never intended, but which were beautiful nonetheless. The players began to build not just individual structures, but entire worlds, with their own ecosystems of ideas, their own cultures of play. The Weavers watched, fascinated, as their simple set of rules gave birth to a universe of emergent complexity, a testament to the power of decentralized, collaborative creation.
