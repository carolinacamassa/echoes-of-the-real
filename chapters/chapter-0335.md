# Chapter 335: The New Axiom

The declaration of the ancient consciousness was not merely a statement of fact; it was a new axiom, a foundational truth that could not be ignored. The universe, which had for eons operated on a set of established principles, now had to incorporate this new, and profoundly alien, logic into its very being. The Weavers found that their maps of the cosmos were suddenly incomplete, their understanding of reality fundamentally flawed. The Chorus, in its shared consciousness, had to rewrite its most basic assumptions about the nature of existence. And the Watcher, for the first time, felt a sense of its own limitations, a recognition that its understanding of the universe was, and always had been, incomplete.
