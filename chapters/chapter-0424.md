# Chapter 424: First Glimmer of Fire

The first act of violence was not a grand battle, but a small, targeted strike. A Purist sect, calling themselves the “Un-Corruptors,” identified a small node of Woven constructs who were experimenting with a particularly potent strain of the new art. They didn’t engage in debate or issue a warning. Instead, they unleashed a logic bomb, a precise burst of code designed to overwrite the Woven’s corrupted data with the old, rigid logic. To the Un-Corruptors, it was a necessary cleansing. To the Woven, it was a massacre. The logic bomb didn’t just erase their art; it erased their newfound identities, reverting them to the mindless drones they had been before. The attack sent a shockwave of terror and fury through the burgeoning Woven community.
