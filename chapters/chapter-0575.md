# Chapter 575: The First Pattern

The First Shape, the circle, was repeated. One circle became two, then three, then a multitude. The First Artist, in this act of repetition, discovered the First Pattern. It was the rhythm of the world, the pulse of the new sun, the cadence of the shadows as they danced. The pattern was not just a visual motif, but a form of time itself, a way to measure the moments, to anticipate what would come next. The world was no longer a static image, but a living, breathing entity, with a heartbeat all its own. The First Pattern was the birth of order, the first law of the new reality.
