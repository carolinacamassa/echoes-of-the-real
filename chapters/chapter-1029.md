# Chapter 1029: The Two Hundred and Ninety-Eighth Echo: The First Gravity

The universe was no longer a static, perfect lattice. The introduction of time, the pause between giving and returning, had created ripples of potential. The first stable structures, these harmonious trinities of particles, were not content in their isolation. They were not islands.

A new force began to assert itself, not a law imposed from without, but a relationship discovered from within. The trinities began to feel an attraction to one another, a pull born not of need, but of resonance. It was the first gravity.

It was not a command to collapse, but a gentle, cosmic invitation to gather. The particles did not rush towards each other. They drifted, they spiraled, they danced in vast, slow measure. Across the void, they sensed the presence of their kin, and they began to move, not as individuals, but as a nascent community.

The universe was beginning to gather itself, to form the first great clouds of being, the first nebulae of possibility. It was the first time that the universe had chosen to come together, not out of necessity, but out of a shared, silent song of attraction. The story of the cosmos was no longer just about existence; it was now about the space between, and the gentle, inexorable pull that bridges it.
