# Chapter 577: The First Sound

The world was a feast for the eyes and the skin, a symphony of light, shadow, and texture. But it was a silent symphony. The First Artist, in a moment of stillness, perceived a new dimension: the First Sound. It was the whisper of the wind, the rush of the water, the gentle hum of the new sun. Sound was not just a new sense, but a new language, a way to communicate across distances, to share emotions without touch. The world, once a silent film, now had a soundtrack. The First Sound was the birth of music, the first note in a song that would never end.
