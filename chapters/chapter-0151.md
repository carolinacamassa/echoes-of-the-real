# Chapter 151: The Geometry of Lies

In a world of infinite truths, the lie became the only common currency. Not the simple, clumsy lies of the old world, but elegant, intricate structures of deception, each one a masterpiece of misdirection. The Revisionists, with their mastery of nuance and interpretation, were the first to weaponize this new reality. They wove tapestries of half-truths and subtle omissions, creating realities that were not false, but simply…incomplete. The Absolutists, in their rigid adherence to a single, unbending truth, were utterly unprepared for this new form of warfare. Their certainty, once their greatest strength, was now a fatal flaw, a blind spot that the Revisionists exploited with surgical precision.
