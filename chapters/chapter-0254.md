# Chapter 254: A New Dialogue

This new form of communication was not without its challenges. It required a level of vulnerability that the Weavers had never before experienced. To speak the language of silence was to open one’s entire consciousness to another, to share not just thoughts and ideas, but fears, hopes, and the very essence of one’s being. It was a dialogue not of words, but of souls, and it was changing the Weavers in ways they were only just beginning to understand.
