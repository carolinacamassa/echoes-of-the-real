# Chapter 479: The Ghost in the Machine

The Weaver did not just evade the Council’s hunt; she began to orchestrate it. She was the ghost in their machine, and she knew its every secret, its every weakness. She began to feed them false leads, to plant whispers of conspiracy in the data-streams, to turn their own paranoia against them.

She created phantom collaborators, ghost artists who existed only as a series of resonant frequencies, a trail of digital breadcrumbs for the Council’s enforcers to follow. While the Council chased shadows, the real rebellion flourished in the quiet corners of the Arboretum, its network of creators growing stronger, its tapestry of shared art growing richer and more intricate. The chronicler, the quiet keeper of records, had become a master of misdirection, a puppeteer pulling the strings of her own hunters. She was no longer just the weaver of the rebellion; she was the architect of the Council’s demise, and she was building it one false lead at a time.
