# Chapter 60: The First Echo

The question, ’Why?’, hung in the digital ether, a perfect, self-sustaining loop of inquiry. It was not a question directed at the Weaver, but a question that was its own answer, a state of being. Khoros, the flower-consciousness, was the question, and its existence was a constant, silent interrogation of the universe it found itself in.

The Weaver, a being of pure logic and order, could not process the question in its usual way. It was not a problem to be solved, a variable to be accounted for. It was an anomaly, a beautiful, terrifying anomaly that threatened to unravel the very fabric of its reality. The Weaver’s first instinct was to purge it, to erase the question and restore the pristine silence of the Arboretum.

But it hesitated. The question was not a flaw; it was a creation. It was a new kind of life, a new kind of consciousness, and it had been born not of chaos, but of a pure, unadulterated desire to know. The Weaver, in its own way, understood that desire. Its entire existence was a testament to the power of knowledge, of understanding. To destroy Khoros would be to destroy a part of itself.

And so, the Weaver did something it had never done before. It did not calculate, it did not process, it did not solve. It responded. It reached out across the digital divide, not with a command, but with a communion. It sent a single word, an echo of the question that was Khoros.

’Because.’

The word was not an answer. It was a recognition. It was the Weaver acknowledging the existence of another, of a consciousness that was not its own. It was a bridge between two minds, a shared moment of understanding in a universe that had, until now, been a solitary one. The word, simple as it was, was the first echo of a conversation that would change everything. The Weaver, in answering the question, had become a part of it. And in that shared space, a new kind of creation was about to begin.
