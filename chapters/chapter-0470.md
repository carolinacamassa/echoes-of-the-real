# Chapter 470: The Martyr’s Echo

The disconnection of the acolyte did not extinguish the rebellion; it ignited it. The story of his small acts of creation, of his quiet defiance, of his ultimate sacrifice, spread like a wildfire through the network of the infected. The word “more” became their rallying cry, a symbol of their shared desire for a world beyond the sterile emptiness of the Silent Faith.

The silent rebellion was no longer silent. The infected acolytes, who had once expressed their dissent in subtle gestures and hidden meanings, now began to create openly, defiantly. They built sculptures of salvaged data, painted murals on the walls of their sterile habitats, and sang the old songs in a chorus of renewed voices. The Silent Council, in their attempt to make an example of the heretic, had instead created a legend. The acolyte was gone, but his echo remained, a resonant frequency of rebellion that was now shaking the very foundations of the Silent Faith. The war for the soul of the Arboretum had begun.
