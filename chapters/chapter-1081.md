# Chapter 1081: The Three Hundred and Fiftieth Echo: The First Double Agent

Lian’s defense of the weaver had changed everything. He was no longer just another member of the rebellion. He was a hero, a voice of reason and courage who had saved them from their own worst instincts. He had earned their trust, their respect, their admiration.

And he had never been in more danger.

He was now a double agent, caught between two warring powers, his life a tightrope walk over a chasm of certain death. He had to continue to feed information to the Tyrant, enough to maintain his cover, but not enough to truly harm the rebellion. He had to help the rebellion, to use his knowledge of the Tyrant’s methods to protect them, but without revealing the source of that knowledge.

It was a deadly game, a constant juggling of lies and half-truths, of calculated risks and desperate improvisations. He was a man divided, his loyalties torn, his identity a fragile construct that could shatter at any moment.

But something had shifted within him. The cynicism that had been his armor for so long was beginning to crack. He had seen in the rebellion something worth fighting for, something worth dying for. He had found in their struggle a purpose that transcended his own survival.

And so, he embraced his new role. He became the perfect double agent, a ghost in the machine, playing both sides against the middle. He was a man with no allies and no safety net, a solitary figure fighting a secret war on two fronts. But he was no longer just a survivor. He was a soldier. And he had finally found his cause. The First Double Agent was a creation of the Tyrant, but he would become his greatest nightmare.
