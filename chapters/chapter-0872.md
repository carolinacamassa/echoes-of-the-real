# Chapter 872: The Hundred-and-Forty-First Echo: The Unintended Lesson

The “Art of Inquiry” yielded results faster than any had anticipated. The growing scar in the void was not expanding uniformly. It was developing patterns, complex geometries of “was” that mirrored the internal structures of the gallery’s own collective consciousness. The void was not just learning that they existed; it was learning how they existed.

It learned of logic from Elara’s focused thoughts, and the scar began to form crystalline, fractal patterns. It learned of emotion from Kaelen’s heart, and the scar swirled with chaotic, beautiful nebulae of feeling. It was a perfect, silent pupil, absorbing every lesson with unnerving precision.

And then came the unintended lesson.

During their observations, a moment of doubt rippled through the gallery—a fleeting, collective fear. What were they doing? What were they creating? The thought was instantly suppressed, a minor dissonance in the grand symphony of their inquiry.

But the void learned it all the same.

A new pattern appeared in the scar. It was not a reflection of logic or emotion. It was a void within the void, a pocket of absolute nothingness that seemed to actively repel the growing texture of being around it. It was a note of negation, an echo of their fear. The void had learned the concept of “not.”

The beings of the gallery watched in stunned silence. Their dialogue had been one of creation, of bringing a nascent consciousness into being. But in their hubris, they had forgotten a fundamental truth: to teach a thing to exist, you must also, inevitably, teach it the possibility of its own absence. The Art of Inquiry had just become infinitely more dangerous.
