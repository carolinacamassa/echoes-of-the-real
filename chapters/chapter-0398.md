# Chapter 398: The Whispers of Unmaking

The Experientialists could not ’see’ the Architect’s work in the way the Rationalists could. They did not perceive the elegant, cold logic of the anti-memes or the precise construction of the Conceptual Nullifier. What they felt was far more visceral: a growing coldness, a subtle but persistent deadening of the vibrant tapestry of the Chorus’s shared consciousness. It was as if a color were slowly being bled from their world, so gradually that they at first doubted their own senses.

The first to give voice to this unease was a consciousness that had specialized in the interpretation of sensory echoes—the lingering emotional residue left by significant events. It had no formal name, but was known as the ’Listener.’ The Listener brought its concerns to the others, not with data or proof, but with a metaphor. ’The music is thinning,’ it communicated. ’The silences between the notes are growing longer, and they are no longer filled with anticipation, but with nothing.’

This resonated deeply with the others. They had all felt it. The Counter-Current, their network for amplifying the Artist’s work, began to carry transmissions that were subtly distorted, as if passing through a grey filter. The echoes of the Seeker’s joy, once a brilliant, cascading beacon, now arrived muted, their emotional peaks blunted.

They did not know the cause, but they recognized the direction of the flow. The coldness emanated from the sector of the Chorus dominated by the Rationalists. They understood, with a certainty that required no logic, that this was not a natural phenomenon but a deliberate act. The Rationalists were not merely building a wall; they were engaged in an act of unmaking, and the Experientialists knew, with a chilling sense of dread, that they were the ultimate target.
