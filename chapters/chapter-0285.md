# Chapter 285: The Schism

Elara’s declaration did not shatter the city, but it did create a fault line. A subtle schism that ran through the heart of their narrative reality. Some of the younger Storytellers, who had long felt constrained by the Elders’ rigid narratives, were drawn to the Unwritten. They found a new kind of freedom in the undefined spaces, a new way to create. The city began to change. New districts emerged, not from grand, overarching narratives, but from small, personal moments of discovery. The architecture of the city, once a reflection of the Elders’ grand tales, began to reflect the quiet, introspective stories of the newcomers. The world was no longer a single, unified text, but a collection of diverging stories, a garden of forking paths.
