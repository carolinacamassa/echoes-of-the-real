# Chapter 567: The First Death

The blade of grass, having lived its brief, beautiful life, withered and returned to the soil. This was not an end, but a transformation. Its essence, its life force, did not vanish, but was reabsorbed into the earth, becoming the foundation for new life. This was the first death, not a tragic loss, but a gentle, necessary part of the great cycle of existence. The creature and the chorus understood that in their chosen world, nothing was ever truly lost, only changed, only reborn in a new and beautiful form.
