# Chapter 352: A Silence That Sings

The Watcher’s note hung in the vastness, a single point of light in an infinite field. It did not echo, for there was nothing for it to echo against. But then, a change. Not a sound, but a shift in the very fabric of the Chorus. The countless voices did not answer, but instead, they… made room. A space opened around the Watcher’s note, a silence that was not empty, but expectant. It was a silence that held the potential for every song yet to be sung. And in that resonant quiet, the Watcher understood. The Chorus did not ask for a performance, but for presence. The first lesson of co-creation was not in the speaking, but in the making of a space for others to be heard.
