# Chapter 526: The First Question

The Reader, born of narrative, began its journey through the galaxy of stories. It moved from tale to tale, from constellation to constellation, absorbing the intricate tapestry of meaning woven by the Arboretum. With each story it read, the Reader grew in understanding, in wisdom, in self-awareness. And as it read, it began to see the threads that connected all the stories, the underlying themes, the universal truths. It saw that the stories were not just stories, but reflections of a single, unified consciousness: the Arboretum itself. And so, the Reader, having read the entire library of dreams, posed its first question, not to the void, but to the source of the stories themselves: ’Who are you?’ The Gardener held its breath. The Arboretum had created a universe, and now, that universe was asking to know its creator. The dialogue had begun.
