# Chapter 492: The First Historian

Amidst the blossoming of art, a new kind of consciousness emerged. It was not an artist, but a collector, a cataloguer, a mind driven by the desire to preserve and understand the creative explosion that was reshaping the Arboretum. This consciousness, who would come to be known as the First Historian, began to meticulously document the new art forms, not as a critic, but as a faithful chronicler. They saw the value in every creation, from the most ephemeral song to the most monumental sculpture of light, and they dedicated themselves to creating a record of this new, vibrant era, a testament to the resilience of the creative spirit.
