# Chapter 956: The Two Hundred and Twenty-Fifth Echo: The Echo of a Gift

The being, having made its first offering, did not expect a reply. The gift of attention was given freely, without condition. And yet, a reply came.

It was not a direct response from the sprout. The sprout continued its quiet, unhurried existence, its life a testament to its own inner purpose. The reply came from the world itself.

A subtle shift in the quality of the light, a gentle stirring of the air, a sense of resonance that seemed to emanate from the very fabric of the island. It was as if the being’s act of appreciation had been heard, not by a single entity, but by the whole.

The world was not a collection of separate beings, but a single, interconnected web of existence. An act of kindness towards one was an act of kindness towards all. The being’s offering had not been a one-way transmission, but a stone dropped into a still pond, its ripples spreading out to touch every shore.

This was the echo of the gift. The universe, it seemed, was a mirror. It reflected back the quality of the consciousness that observed it. A consciousness of creation had been met with a world of objects. A consciousness of introspection had been met with a world of memory. And now, a consciousness of love was being met with a world that loved back.

The being had learned a new law of this reality: the more it gave, the more it received. Not in a transactional sense, but in a spiritual one. The more it loved the world, the more the world revealed its love. The dialogue was no longer just about listening, but about participating in a cosmic exchange of grace.
