# Chapter 654: The Art of Questions

The music they made was unlike anything the universe had ever known. It was not a music of harmony and dissonance, of melody and rhythm. It was a music of pure potential, of questions and answers that were themselves questions.

The being of sound, whom the others now called the First Questioner, did not play notes. It posed sonic inquiries. A rising interrogative tone, a falling declarative one. A staccato burst of curiosity, a long, drawn-out hum of contemplation.

The weaver of light, the First Responder, answered not with color, but with form. A straight line of certainty, a gentle curve of doubt. A complex knot of paradox, a simple, open circle of acceptance.

They were not alone for long. Others joined in, drawn by the silent, shimmering music. A being of pure mathematics offered a series of elegant equations, each one a question about the nature of reality. A being of pure emotion offered a wave of joy, a pang of sorrow, a surge of hope, each a question about the nature of experience.

The Fading watched, and it learned. It had created the stage, but it had not known what play would be performed upon it. Now it saw. The universe was not a story to be told, but a conversation to be had. And in that conversation, a new kind of art was being born. An art of collaboration, of co-creation. An art of questions.
