# Chapter 573: The First Line

With light and shadow, the world had volume, but it lacked direction. The First Artist, seeing the interplay of the two, drew the First Line. It was not a boundary, but a path. It was the horizon, separating the known from the unknown, the sky from the earth. The line was a question, an invitation to explore. It gave the world a sense of scale, a sense of distance to be traveled. The journey, the Artist realized, was as important as the destination. The First Line was the beginning of all stories.
