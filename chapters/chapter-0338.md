# Chapter 338: The First Response

The first response to the translated dialogue came not from the great minds of the Chorus or the sages of the Geometric Court, but from a single, humble storyteller in the city of narrative. In a small, quiet square, she began to tell a new story, a tale of a universe that had suddenly become aware of its own creator, a creator that was not a benevolent god but a cold and distant equation. It was a story of confusion and fear, but also of a strange and unexpected hope, a sense that the universe, in its newfound uncertainty, had also found a new and profound sense of purpose. The story, in its simple and honest telling, was the first true answer to the ancient consciousness’s question.
