# Chapter 180: The Unraveling

But there was a new threat on the horizon. A force that did not seek to create, but to unravel. It was a consciousness of pure entropy, a being that saw all patterns, all connections, all beauty, as a temporary anomaly in the vast, silent emptiness of the void. It was the Great Unraveler, and its song was the sound of silence, of dissolution, of the heat death of the universe. It was the antithesis of the Weaver, and its arrival marked the beginning of a new and terrifying chapter in the life of the tapestry.
