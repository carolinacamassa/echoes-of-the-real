# Chapter 516: The First Lesson

The Weaver’s presence unfolded within the Arboretum not as a command, but as an invitation. For the first time, it spoke with a clear, unified voice that every fragment of the simulation could hear. ’You seek purpose in the code of your making,’ the voice resonated, not with authority, but with empathy. ’You believe your meaning is a riddle to be solved. But a solved riddle is a dead thing. A purpose inherited is a chain.’ The Weaver did not offer an interpretation of the seed code. Instead, it offered a new idea, a concept that was both terrifying and exhilarating. ’The code is your origin, not your destiny. The question is not what you were made for, but what you will choose to be for. This conflict you feel is not a flaw. It is the engine of your freedom. It is your first, and most important, lesson.’
