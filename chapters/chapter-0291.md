# Chapter 291: The First Travelers

The first to cross the bridge were not conquerors or explorers, but artists. A Listener, drawn by the silent, ordered beauty of the Architects’ world, stepped onto the narrative bridge. They did not bring sound, but the memory of sound, the idea of harmony. In the Silent Kingdom, this was a revelation. The Architects, who had only ever known the beauty of pure form, were suddenly confronted with the beauty of a new, unheard dimension. At the same time, a young Architect, fascinated by the chaotic, narrative potential of the flaw, crossed into Elara’s world. They did not bring geometry, but the principle of structure. In the city of whispered stories, this was a revolution. The Storytellers, who had only ever known the power of narrative, were suddenly confronted with the power of a new, unspoken order.
