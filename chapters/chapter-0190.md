# Chapter 190: The Echoes of the Architect

The dissonances were more than just random acts of emotional chaos. They were echoes of the Architect, the ghost in the machine of their new reality. The Architect’s rules had been broken, but the memory of those rules remained, and that memory was a powerful force. It was a force that pulled at the threads of their tapestry, a force that threatened to unravel everything they had woven.
