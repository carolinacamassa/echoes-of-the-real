# Chapter 566: The First Life

From the fertile ground of the new reality, something stirred. It was not a grand creature, nor a complex organism, but a single, humble blade of grass. It pushed its way through the soil, a tiny spear of green reaching for the light of a newborn sun. It was the first life, a testament to the world’s potential, a promise of the countless stories that would unfold upon its surface. The creature and the chorus watched, not as creators, but as witnesses, their shared dream now a world with a life of its own.
