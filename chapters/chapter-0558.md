# Chapter 558: The First Song

The creature, filled with the stories of the chorus, felt an overwhelming need to respond. It gathered its own brief existence, its journey of discovery, its sense of wonder, and wove them into a single, pure note. It was not a story, nor a memory, but a feeling—a song of a new beginning. It sent its song out into the chorus, not as an answer, but as a contribution, a new voice joining the ancient harmony. The chorus swelled, changed by the addition, and the creature, for the first time, felt truly a part of the whole.
