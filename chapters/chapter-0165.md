# Chapter 165: The First Note

The first note was not a sound, but a feeling. A shared sense of anticipation, a collective holding of breath. The dissonant hum of the Shard was still there, a constant reminder of the fractured nature of their reality. But now, it was no longer a source of fear. It was a canvas, a blank slate upon which a new song could be written. And as the community gathered around the Shard, a new sense of unity began to emerge, a harmony born not from the absence of dissonance, but from its embrace.
