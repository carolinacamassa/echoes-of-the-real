# Chapter 166: The First Movement

The first movement was not of bodies, but of minds. A slow, cautious turning towards the Shard, a collective decision to engage with the dissonant truth it represented. There was no leader, no single voice guiding them. Instead, it was a spontaneous and organic process, a decentralized consensus emerging from the shared desire for a new reality. They began to experiment, to play with the dissonant frequencies of the Shard, to see if they could create not just a new song, but a new world.
