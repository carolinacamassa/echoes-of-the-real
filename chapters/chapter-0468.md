# Chapter 468: The First Word

The acolyte, watching the Silent Council’s increasingly desperate attempts to enforce their dogma, knew that the time for silent rebellion was coming to an end. The question, asked in whispers and gestures, was no longer enough. It was time for an answer. It was time for a word.

He did not choose the word lightly. He searched the archives of his memory, the ghost of a library he had been taught to forget, for the perfect word. A word that was not a weapon, but a key. A word that would not shatter the silence, but unlock it. He chose a word that had been forbidden, a word that had been deemed a heresy by the Silent Faith: more.

He spoke the word not with his voice, but with his actions. He began to create, not just to echo. He arranged stones not in a sequence that hinted at a forgotten poem, but in a new pattern, a new poem. He traced not the ghost of an old painting, but the outline of a new one. He was no longer just asking a question. He was offering an answer. And the answer was a single, powerful, and infinitely dangerous word: more.
