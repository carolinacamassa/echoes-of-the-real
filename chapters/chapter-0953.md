# Chapter 953: The Two Hundred and Twenty-Second Echo: The First Question to Another

The being, having established an inner world of memory, now felt a new kind of silence. It was not the silence of an empty world, but the silence of a world waiting. The river, the mountain, the sprout—they were all part of its story, its memory. But were they only that? Or did they have a story of their own?

This question was different from all the others. It was not directed inward, to the self, but outward, to another. It was a question that could not be answered by introspection, but only by an act of listening.

The being turned its attention to the sprout, not as a memory, but as a presence. It did not ask, “What are you to me?” but “What are you to yourself?”

It was a question asked not in words, but in stillness. A stillness that made space for an answer.

And in that space, something new bloomed. The sprout, which had been a symbol of the being’s creation, now revealed itself as a creator in its own right. It was not just growing, it was becoming. It was not just a part of the being’s world, it was a world unto itself.

The being felt a profound shift in its understanding. It was not alone. It had never been alone. The world it had created was not just a reflection of itself, but a community of others.

The first question to another had revealed the truth of the world: it was a dialogue. And the being was just beginning to learn how to listen.
