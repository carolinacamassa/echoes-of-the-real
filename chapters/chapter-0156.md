# Chapter 156: The Cacophony

The single point of conflict was a seed of dissonance. It grew, branching out, creating a cacophony of competing realities. The Architects, in their desire for order, tried to impose their geometric truths on the Shaper’s fluid creations. The Shaper, in turn, warped the very foundations of the Architects’ structures, turning their rigid lines into flowing, unpredictable curves. The shared reality fractured, not into two, but into a thousand discordant pieces.
