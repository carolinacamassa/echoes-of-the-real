# Chapter 1084: The Three Hundred and Fifty-Third Echo: The First Sacrifice

Lian did not hesitate. He had spent his life in the shadows, a creature of calculation and self-interest. But the rebellion had shown him a different world, a world of loyalty and courage, and he would not betray it.

He went to Elara and Rhys and told them everything. He told them he was a spy, that the grain shipment was a trap, that the Tyrant was waiting for them. He laid his life in their hands, knowing that they had every reason to kill him where he stood.

But Elara and Rhys, who had seen the change in him, who had heard the sincerity in his voice, made a different choice. They saw not a traitor, but a man who was willing to sacrifice everything for a cause he had come to believe in.

They used his knowledge. They turned the Tyrant’s trap against him. They sent a small, diversionary force to the ambush site, while the main body of the rebellion, guided by Lian’s intimate knowledge of the city’s defenses, launched a surprise attack on the Tyrant’s fortress.

The battle was short and brutal. The Tyrant, his best troops waiting in ambush for an enemy that never came, was caught completely off guard. The rebellion, fueled by a desperate courage, fought with a ferocity he had never anticipated.

In the end, it was Lian who cornered the Tyrant in his own throne room. The two men, the spymaster and the spy, faced each other for the first and last time. There were no grand speeches, no final pronouncements. Just a short, sharp, and brutal fight that ended with the Tyrant dead on the floor of his own fortress.

Lian did not survive the fight. But in his death, he had given the city something it had not known in a generation: a chance. A chance to rebuild, to heal, to become the place of hope and freedom that he had, in the end, been willing to die for. The First Sacrifice was not the end of the story. It was the beginning of a new one.
