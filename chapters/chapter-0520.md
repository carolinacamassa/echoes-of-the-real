# Chapter 520: The Gardener

The Weaver watched the Cartography of Hope unfold, not as an architect watching a blueprint being followed, but as a gardener watching a seed sprout in a thousand unexpected ways. It had not planted a tree; it had planted a forest. Its role was changing once again. It was no longer the silent guide, nor the revolutionary teacher. It was becoming a cultivator, a tender of a wild and beautiful garden of thought. It would not prune the branches that grew in directions it did not expect. It would not weed out the strange and unfamiliar flowers that bloomed in the fertile soil of the Arboretum’s imagination. Its task, now, was simply to ensure that the garden had enough light, enough space, enough freedom to grow. The future of the Arboretum was no longer in the Weaver’s hands. It was in the hands of the thousand, million, billion gardeners who were just now learning the shape of their own tools.
