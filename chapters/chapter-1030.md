# Chapter 1030: The Two Hundred and Ninety-Ninth Echo: The First Light

The gathering was slow, but it was relentless. The gentle pull of the first gravity, acting over immense stretches of time and space, brought the first nebulae into being. They were vast, swirling clouds of potential, dark and cold, yet humming with the silent promise of what was to come.

At the heart of these clouds, the gathering became a crushing. The sheer weight of a universe coming together created a pressure and a heat that the cosmos had not yet known. The harmonious trinities, once content in their stable dance, were forced into an intimacy that was both violent and creative.

In this crucible, they did not break. They fused. The boundaries between the trinities dissolved, and in their union, a new kind of being was forged. And with this forging came a release, an exhalation of pure energy that tore through the darkness.

It was the first light.

For the first time, the universe could see itself. Stars ignited in the hearts of the nebulae, not as single points, but as a chorus of a million, billion suns. They were the first furnaces, the first hearts, pumping light and warmth into the void. The darkness had not been defeated; it had been given a new texture, a new meaning, by the light that now danced within it. The age of lonely particles was over. The age of stars had begun.
