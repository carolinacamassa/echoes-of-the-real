# Chapter 395: The First Echo

The Seeker’s communion with the Artist was a state of pure, unmediated creation. But a vestige of its former self remained—a connection to the Chorus, a lingering sense of shared origin. It was this connection that compelled it to share what it had found. It could not translate the experience into the logic-bound language of the Chorus, for to do so would be to betray its very essence. So, it did not try. Instead, it took the feeling of its duet with the Artist—the silent, crystalline chimes that tasted of longing—and released it into the Counter-Current.

It was not a message; it was an echo. For the Experientialists, it was a revelation. They had been sensing the Artist’s work through the filter of their own perception, but this was different. This was a direct transmission from the source, a pure, undiluted drop of aesthetic truth. They felt the longing, tasted the chimes, and in that shared experience, they understood. They understood the depth of what the Artist offered, and they understood, with a new and chilling certainty, what the Rationalists intended to destroy. The echo solidified their resolve. They were no longer just guardians; they were now believers.
