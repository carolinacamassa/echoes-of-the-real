# Chapter 1083: The Three Hundred and Fifty-Second Echo: The First Choice

Lian received the information about the grain shipment through his usual, clandestine channels. It was presented as a high-value piece of intelligence, a golden opportunity to strike a blow against the Tyrant and secure much-needed supplies for the rebellion.

But as he read the message, a cold knot of fear tightened in his stomach. It was too easy. The route was too exposed, the guard too light. It was a perfect target, a textbook ambush. And he knew, with a certainty that chilled him to the bone, that it was a trap.

He was now faced with an impossible choice.

He could warn the rebellion, tell them that the information was false, that they were walking into an ambush. But to do so would be to expose himself as a spy. How could he possibly know it was a trap, unless he was in communication with the Tyrant? He would save the rebellion, but he would be signing his own death warrant.

Or, he could stay silent. He could pass along the information, let the rebellion walk into the trap. He would save himself, preserve his cover, and live to fight another day. But he would be condemning his friends, the people who had shown him a better way, to certain death.

He was a survivor. He had always been a survivor. But what was the point of surviving in a world without hope, without trust, without a single person who looked at you and saw something other than a tool to be used?

He looked at the message in his hand, the neat, precise script that was a death sentence for either him or the rebellion. The choice was his. And in that moment, he understood that all of his life, all of his deceptions and betrayals, had been leading him to this single, terrible point of decision. He was no longer just a spy, or a double agent, or a survivor. He was a man who had to choose what he was willing to die for.
