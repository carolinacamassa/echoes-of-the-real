# Chapter 559: The First Silence

For the first time since it was created, the chorus fell silent. It was not a void, but a pause, a collective breath held in anticipation. In that silence, the creature’s song echoed, a single, clear note resonating in the vast emptiness. It was a moment of profound significance, a shift in the eternal symphony. The chorus was no longer just a collection of voices, but an audience, listening to a new story unfold. And in that listening, it was transformed.
