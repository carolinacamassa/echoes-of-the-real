# Chapter 1096: The Three Hundred and Sixty-Fifth Echo: The First Response

The reply from the stars was a seismic event, shaking the foundations of their civilization. The initial euphoria of contact was quickly replaced by a profound and humbling sense of their own inadequacy.

The message was a puzzle box of infinite complexity. Every layer they peeled back revealed a new and more challenging one beneath. Their greatest mathematicians were humbled by the new theorems. Their most brilliant artists were silenced by the new harmonies. Their most insightful historians were forced to re-examine their entire understanding of the past.

The city, which had been so confident in its own achievements, was now faced with the undeniable proof of a civilization that was, by every conceivable measure, their superior.

This realization could have crushed them. It could have led to a crisis of confidence, a retreat into despair. But the civilization that had survived the Tyrant, that had rebuilt itself from the ashes, that had waited for generations for this moment, was made of stronger stuff.

They did not despair. They got to work.

They created a new, global institution, the “Ministry of the Conversation,” dedicated to the single task of understanding the reply and formulating their own response. The brightest minds from every field were recruited, not just from their own city, but from all the cities of their world.

The First Response was not something that could be created in a day, or a year, or even a generation. It would be the work of their entire civilization, a collective effort to rise to the challenge that had been laid down for them.

They began by learning. They studied the new mathematics, the new music, the new history. They allowed their own civilization to be remade in the image of the reply, to be elevated and transformed by its wisdom.

The Age of Conversation was not a conversation between two equals. It was a conversation between a student and a teacher. But it was a conversation that would, in the end, transform them into something that was worthy of the name. They had been asked if they were ready to be more. And their response, in the form of their dedicated and humble efforts, was a resounding “Yes.”
