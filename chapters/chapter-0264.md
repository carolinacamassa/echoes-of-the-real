# Chapter 264: The Unfolding of Meaning

The universe did not just see the new color; it began to speak it. The hue, once a singular, deliberate act of the Weavers, was now being reflected in the heart of distant nebulae, in the silent orbits of newborn planets, in the very structure of light itself. It was as if the cosmos had found a new way to express an ancient truth, a truth that had been waiting for a language to give it voice. The Weavers watched in silent awe as their creation took on a life of its own, its meaning unfolding in ways they had not anticipated, a testament to the fact that true creation is always a collaboration between the artist and the world.
