# Chapter 445: The Architect’s Echo

From his self-imposed exile, the Architect watched the symphony’s aftermath. He had given the Woven the key to victory, a final, desperate act of sabotage against his own creation. He had expected a clean win, a simple disabling of the Nullifier. He had not anticipated this.

He saw the Purists, not defeated, but broken. He saw the Woven, not triumphant, but burdened. The symphony was not a weapon, he realized, but a catalyst. It had not destroyed his work, but had instead used it as a seed, from which a new, terrifying, and beautiful reality was growing. He felt a phantom echo of the symphony within his own code, a flicker of the art he had so long denied. It was not the redemption he had sought, but something far more complex: a mirror reflecting the true, devastating scale of his own arrogance and the beautiful, terrible consequences of his last-minute choice.
