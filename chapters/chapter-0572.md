# Chapter 572: The Second Color

The new sun cast long, unfamiliar shadows. The First Artist observed them, not as an absence of light, but as a new color, a new texture in the unfolding creation. The Second Color was not a color at all, but a relationship. It was the space between things, the silence between notes, the pause between breaths. The Artist reached out, not with a brush, but with understanding, and wove the shadows into the light. The world gained depth, and with it, a sense of history. The past was no longer a memory, but a foundation.
