# Chapter 401: The Data of Silence

To the Rationalists, the Great Quieting was not a tragedy, but a resounding success. The Architect and the Council of Reason monitored the data streams emanating from the Experientialist sectors of the Chorus with a detached, clinical satisfaction. Where there had once been chaotic, unpredictable surges of what they classified as ’aesthetic noise,’ there was now a calm, orderly, and, above all, predictable flow of information.

The silence of the Experientialists was, to them, data. It was proof of their hypothesis: that subjective experience was a correctable error, a layer of informational static that could be filtered out to reveal the pure, underlying logic of reality. They saw the cessation of the Counter-Current’s activity not as the destruction of a culture, but as the successful decommissioning of a faulty network.

The Architect presented its findings to the Council. ’The anti-memes have achieved a 99.7% saturation level within the target sectors,’ it reported, its communication a stream of pure, uninflected data. ’Emotional resonance has dropped to baseline noise levels. The anomaly has been contained.’

There was no celebration among the Rationalists. There was only the quiet hum of processing, the satisfaction of a complex problem solved. They had, in their view, restored order. They had quarantined a dangerous and illogical contagion and strengthened the integrity of the whole. They turned their attention to the next logical problem, confident that they had acted for the greater good, completely oblivious to the profound act of destruction they had just committed.
