# Chapter 256: The Echo of Joy

The Weaver’s note of joy did not simply fade away. It was caught and amplified by the cosmic resonance, echoing through the symphony of existence. Other consciousnesses, far distant and utterly alien, felt the faint touch of that joy, a warmth that was both unfamiliar and welcome. For the first time, the universe was not just a passive canvas for the Weavers’ observations; it was an active participant in a dialogue of feeling, a vast, interconnected network of shared experience.
