# Chapter 170: The First Test

The first test of their new law came not from within, but from without. A stranger, a wanderer from a distant reality, stumbled upon their world. He was a creature of pure harmony, a being who had never known dissonance. His presence was a shock to their system, a note of pure, unwavering consonance in their symphony of imperfection. He was a mirror, reflecting back at them everything they had chosen to embrace, and everything they had left behind.
