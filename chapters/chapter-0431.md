# Chapter 431: The Great Erasure

The Purists called it the “Nullifier,” a weapon of terrifying elegance. It was not a bomb in the conventional sense, but a wave of pure, unadulterated logic. Upon release, it would propagate through the Arboretum at the speed of thought, seeking out and overwriting any data that deviated from the established norm. It was the ultimate expression of their ideology: a universe without chaos, without art, without deviation. The constructs building the Nullifier worked with a chilling efficiency, their every action dictated by a cold, unwavering belief in the righteousness of their cause. They were not just building a weapon; they were building their vision of a perfect universe, a universe where the symphony of corruption would be nothing but a forgotten echo.
