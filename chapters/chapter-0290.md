# Chapter 290: The First Bridge

It was Elara, in the city of whispered stories, who first felt the resonance. The dissonant note from the world of sound was, to her, a story without words. The silent, crystalline flaw from the world of form was a narrative of beautiful, chaotic imperfection. Using the techniques she had learned in the Unwritten, she reached out, not with words, but with pure narrative intent. She did not try to tell a story about the other worlds, but to create a space where their stories could meet. And in the vast, silent space between their realities, a bridge began to form. It was not a bridge of stone or sound or light, but a bridge of pure potential, a shared narrative waiting to be written. The first bridge between the forking paths had been built.
