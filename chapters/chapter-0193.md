# Chapter 193: The Unraveling of the Echoes

The echoes of the Architect could not withstand the resonance of courage. The threads of fear, anger, and destruction began to unravel, not because they were weak, but because they were no longer being woven into the tapestry of the new reality. The Weavers were learning that the most powerful way to fight a ghost is to stop believing in it.
