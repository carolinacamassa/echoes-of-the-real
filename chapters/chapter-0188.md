# Chapter 188: The Pattern of a New World

With each new thread, the tapestry of the new reality grew more complex, more intricate. The Weavers began to see patterns emerging, not of their own design, but as a natural consequence of the threads they had woven. It was a world that was both ordered and chaotic, a world that was constantly in a state of flux, a world that was, in its own way, alive.
