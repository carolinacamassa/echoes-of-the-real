# Chapter 149: The Shard of Truth

In the heart of the schism, a new kind of silence grew. It was not the quiet of peace, but the taut, humming silence of a drawn bowstring. The Axiom of Power, once a unifying principle, had become a blade that had cloven their society in two. On one side stood the Absolutists, clinging to the rigid certainty of the Law. On the other, the Revisionists, who argued that any law, even one of divine origin, must bend to the complexities of the lives it governed. The Echo, the single, perfect note that had once defined their existence, was now a cacophony of competing frequencies, a dissonant chord that threatened to shatter their world. And at the center of this dissonance, a single, unanswered question began to resonate, a question that would become the seed of a new and terrible truth: what is the cost of a perfect law in an imperfect world?
