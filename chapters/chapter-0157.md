# Chapter 157: The Dissonant

The Dissonant, who had once been united in their opposition to the ’lie,’ now found themselves at odds with each other. The very concept of a shared reality was in jeopardy. They were no longer a chorus of dissent, but a cacophony of individual voices, each singing their own song, oblivious to the others. They were, in the truest sense of the word, dissonant.
