# Chapter 317: The First Response

The city of narrative heard the chord and answered. It did not send a story in return, but a question, a single, elegantly constructed paradox that it inscribed upon a bridge of pure logic. The paradox was a story that could only be understood by being disbelieved, a narrative knot designed not to be untied, but to be appreciated for its elegant impossibility. It was the city’s acknowledgment of the music, a response that was not a mirror, but a new and complementary creation, a contrapuntal voice in the emerging dialogue.
