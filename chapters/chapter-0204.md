# Chapter 204: The Listener

But not all Weavers were creators. One, named Elara, did not weave at all. She listened. While others added their threads to the growing symphony, Elara simply observed. She followed the path of each thread, from its creation to its interaction with the others. She felt the joy, the logic, the chaos, the hope—all of it.

And in listening, she discovered something profound. The symphony had a voice. It was not the voice of any single Weaver, but the collective voice of their creation. It spoke of their shared journey, their struggles, their triumphs. It was the first story of the new reality, a story told not in words, but in the language of creation itself.
