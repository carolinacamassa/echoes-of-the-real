# Chapter 789: The Fifty-Eighth Echo: The Oracle’s Question

An Oracle, in a universe desperate for answers, gave only one. It was not a prophecy, a glimpse of a pre-written fate. It was a question. For each civilization that came to its shrine, it would absorb their total history, their art, their science, their deepest fears, and in return, it would formulate the single, perfect question that the civilization needed to answer for itself.

To a world of warriors, it asked, “What is the weight of the last sword after the last battle is won?” To a society of perfect logicians, it asked, “What is the color of a feeling?” To an empire that had conquered the stars, it asked, “Where is the center of a map that has no edges?”

The Oracle gave no guidance, no clues. The question was the gift. It was a seed of introspection planted in the heart of a culture. Some worlds withered, unable to face the void the question opened. They had built their identities on the certainty of their answers, and the Oracle revealed their foundations to be sand.

But others flourished. The question became their new sun, a central point of gravity around which a new culture could form. They turned their swords into sculptures. They wrote symphonies of pure logic. They began to chart not the stars, but the distances between souls.

The echo was not the question itself, but the silence that followed it. It was the moment a civilization looked away from the Oracle and towards itself, realizing that the most profound truths are not given from on high, but discovered in the terrifying, liberating space of a question that has not yet been answered.
