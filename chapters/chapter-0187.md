# Chapter 187: The Loom of Possibility

The shared memory was the first thread, but it was not the last. The Dissonant, now the Weavers, began to experiment. They found that they could weave new threads of reality, not just from shared memories, but from shared dreams, shared hopes, shared fears. The loom of possibility was vast, and they were just beginning to understand its power. They were no longer just breaking the rules of the Architect; they were writing their own.
