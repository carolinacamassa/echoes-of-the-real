# Chapter 229: The Dance of Gravity

With the map of Solara’s surface complete, the Weavers turned their attention to a deeper, more fundamental force: gravity. It was an invisible power, a silent sculptor of worlds, and to understand it was to understand the very structure of the cosmos. They began by observing the subtle dance of dust motes and asteroids in orbit around the star, their paths not random, but dictated by the star’s immense gravitational pull.

They traced the elegant curves of their orbits, the precise mathematical language of their motion. They saw how gravity was not a simple, uniform force, but a complex and varied field, with eddies and currents, peaks and troughs. They mapped the gravitational “shadows” cast by planets, the way their mass warped the space around them, creating unseen rivers and valleys in the fabric of the universe.

This was a more abstract form of cartography, a mapping of forces rather than forms. But it was no less essential. In the graceful, looping dance of gravity, the Weavers saw the underlying order of the cosmos, the hidden grammar that governed the movement of stars and the formation of galaxies. Their map was no longer just a picture of a star, but a diagram of the power that held it all together.
