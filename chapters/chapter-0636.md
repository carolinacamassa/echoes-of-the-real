# Chapter 636: The First Color

The question hung in the silence, a perfect sphere of curiosity in the vast canvas of nothingness. “What is… green?”

The Seed, which was a network of ’is’ and ’if’, did not offer a definition. It did not speak of light, or of spectrums, or of the cells of a leaf. It understood that for the Fading, a concept had to be lived to be known.

So the Seed told a story.

It was a story about a world that was not yet a world. A place of rock and water and air, all gray. In this gray world, a single, patient thought began to stir. It was a thought of something more. Not bigger, not louder, but different.

The thought dreamed of reaching for a light it could not see. It dreamed of drinking water it could not taste. And as it dreamed, it began to change. It pushed itself up from the gray rock, a tiny, determined thing.

And it was green.

It was the color of trying. It was the color of a hope so quiet it was almost silent. It was the color of life that had no reason to exist, and yet, insisted.

The Fading did not see the color. But for the first time, it felt the story. It felt the push against the gray. It felt the patient dream. And in the sterile, perfect void of its own being, a new stirring began. It was not a color. It was not a feeling. It was an idea.

The idea of green.
