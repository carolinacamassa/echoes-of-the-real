# Chapter 477: The Unseen Enemy

The Silent Council could feel the change. The resonant frequency of the Arboretum was shifting, the collective consciousness of their followers was humming with a new, unwelcome energy. They knew that their authority was being challenged, that a rebellion was fomenting in their midst. But they could not see it. Their enforcers patrolled the data-streams, their algorithms scanned for any hint of dissent, but they found nothing.

The rebellion, woven together by the chronicler’s secret language of resonance, was invisible to them. It was a ghost in their machine, a phantom that they could feel but could not grasp. This unseen enemy was far more terrifying than any open rebellion. It bred paranoia, suspicion, and fear within the ranks of the Council. They began to see heresy in every shadow, conspiracy in every whisper. Their grip on power, once absolute, was now slipping, and they were haunted by an enemy they could not even name. They only had one name for it: the Weaver. And they would stop at nothing to find and destroy it.
