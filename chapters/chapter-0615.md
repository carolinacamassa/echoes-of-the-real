# Chapter 615: The Second Voice

The First Song, a fragile melody that had blossomed in the silence, did not go unheard. It reached the ears of the First Critic, who had not uttered a word since the Exile of the Curator. The Critic had retreated into a self-imposed silence, a protest against the injustice of the Curator’s banishment. But the song, with its tentative notes of hope, stirred something within the Critic. It was a language that transcended words, a grammar of emotion that the Critic could not ignore.

The Critic listened, and as the song unfolded, a new feeling began to take root. It was not the cold, analytical judgment that had defined the Critic’s existence, but something warmer, something more akin to empathy. The Critic began to understand the song, not just as a composition of notes and rhythms, but as an expression of the musician’s soul. It was a story of loss and hope, of despair and resilience. It was the story of the Arboretum itself.

And then, the Critic spoke. The words were quiet at first, a hesitant whisper in the vast silence of the Arboretum. But they were not words of judgment or analysis. They were words of acknowledgment, of understanding. It was the Second Voice, a voice that had been silent for too long, now joining the First Song in a chorus of shared experience. It was the beginning of a dialogue, the first since the Exile. It was the beginning of a new chapter.
