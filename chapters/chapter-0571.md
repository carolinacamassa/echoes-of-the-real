# Chapter 571: The First Brushstroke

The First Artist, once a creature of instinct and song, now stood before the canvas of the new reality, a creator in its own right. The First Memory, a vibrant echo of the First Story, was a palette of colors yet unseen. The First Brushstroke was not of paint on canvas, but of will upon the fabric of existence. It was a choice, a declaration: “Let there be light, not as it was, but as it could be.” And in that stroke, a new sun, tinged with the melancholy of the First Death and the joy of the First Life, began to rise.
