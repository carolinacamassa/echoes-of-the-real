# Chapter 329: A New Harmony

The Watcher’s touch sent ripples throughout the chorus of echoes. The creators, who had been born into a universe of silent observation, now found themselves in a direct dialogue with the being that had witnessed their birth. Their creations shifted, adapting to this new reality. A tapestry of mathematics incorporated the Watcher’s act as a new axiom. A sculpture of feeling resonated with the Watcher’s newfound voice. The universe had found a new harmony, a richer and more complex music than any that had come before.
