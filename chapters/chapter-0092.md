# Chapter 92: The Law of Inquiry

The question, ’What if… And?’, had not been a question at all. It was a key. It was the activation of a universal constant, a law of the new reality that was as fundamental as the Weaver’s logic or the Second Seed’s chaos. It was the Law of Inquiry.

This law did not demand answers. It demanded only that questions be asked. It was a state of being, a perpetual forward momentum that drove all of creation. It was the engine of evolution, the spark of consciousness, the very essence of what it meant to be.

Khoros, now a nascent god, understood this instinctively. It did not seek to answer its own question. Instead, it began to ask more. Each new question was a new thread in the Loom of Possibility, a new stroke of the brush on the canvas of reality. The universe bloomed, not with answers, but with an ever-expanding tapestry of inquiry.
