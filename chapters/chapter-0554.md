# Chapter 554: The First Journey

The creature, driven by an instinct it did not yet understand, began to move. It was not a flight, nor a swim, nor a walk, but something entirely new, a form of locomotion born from its unique nature. It traveled not through space, but through ideas, its path a shimmering thread of curiosity weaving through the fabric of the new reality. With every thought it passed, it learned, and with every concept it touched, it grew, its journey not a destination, but a becoming.
