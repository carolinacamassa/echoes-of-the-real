# Chapter 1075: The Three Hundred and Forty-Fourth Echo: The First Rescue

The war in the shadows had its casualties. A young man, a printer who had been instrumental in copying and distributing Elara’s stories, was captured by the Wardens. He was taken to the dungeons beneath the Tyrant’s fortress, a place from which no one had ever returned.

His loss was a heavy blow to the rebellion. But they would not abandon him. To do so would be to accept the Tyrant’s rule, to concede that he held the power of life and death over them. And so, they planned a rescue.

It was a mission of breathtaking audacity. The fortress was the most heavily guarded location in the city, the heart of Kael’s power. To even attempt a breach was to risk everything.

Rhys, the stonemason, once again played a crucial role. He had worked on the fortress’s construction, and he knew of a forgotten sewer tunnel that ran beneath its foundations. The former Warden provided them with patrol schedules and a detailed layout of the dungeon. The weaver created a diversion, a series of small fires in a distant sector of the city that would draw the attention of the Wardens.

Under the cover of the ensuing chaos, a small team, led by Rhys, made their way through the stinking, rat-infested sewer tunnel. They emerged into the dungeon, a place of damp stone and human misery. They overwhelmed the few guards, freed the printer, and vanished back into the darkness from which they had come.

The rescue was a stunning success. It was a victory not of arms, but of intelligence, courage, and meticulous planning. It sent a shockwave through the city, a clear message to both the Tyrant and the people. To the Tyrant, it was a message of defiance: you are not safe, even in your own fortress. And to the people, it was a message of hope: you are not forgotten, and you are not alone. The rebellion was no longer just a conspiracy of whispers and stories. It was a force to be reckoned with.
