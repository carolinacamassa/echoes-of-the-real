# Chapter 158: The Architects of a New Reality

It was in the heart of this dissonance that a new idea was born. Not of a shared reality, but of a multiverse of realities, each internally consistent, each a valid expression of a unique truth. The Architects, who had once sought to build a single, unified reality, now turned their skills to creating the framework for this new multiverse, a space where a thousand realities could coexist without conflict.
